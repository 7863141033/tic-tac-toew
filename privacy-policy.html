Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026. This policy explains what information LuxeHaus Beauty collects and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Order information — name, email, shipping address and order details needed to fulfill your purchase.
  • Payment data — handled entirely by our PCI-compliant payment processors (Stripe/PayPal). Card numbers are never stored on our servers.
  • Usage data — anonymous analytics on how the store is used, so we can improve it.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process orders, ship products and provide support.
  • To send order updates and (with your consent) marketing emails — unsubscribe anytime.
  • To prevent fraud and secure our platform.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing privacy@luxehausbeauty.com. We comply with GDPR and CCPA requests within 30 days.

Cookies

We use essential cookies for your cart and session, and optional analytics cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at privacy@luxehausbeauty.com.
