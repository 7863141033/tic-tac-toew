Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know — shipping, returns, hair care and more.

How long does shipping take?
Standard shipping takes 5–8 business days and is free on orders over $75 ($5.99 below). Express (3–4 days) and Priority Overnight (1–2 days) are available at checkout. All orders ship from our fulfillment center within 24 hours on business days.
What is your return policy?
We offer a 14-day return window from the date of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed and in their original packaging with all lace intact. Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–7 business days of receiving your return.
Can I exchange my item instead?
Yes — exchanges are free within 14 days. If you'd prefer a different length, color or texture, contact us with your order number and we'll hold your exchange while your return ships back. Hair that has been cut, dyed or installed is not eligible.
How long does order processing take?
Orders are processed within 24 hours (Monday–Saturday). You'll receive a confirmation email immediately and a tracking number as soon as your package is scanned by the carrier — usually within 1 business day.
How do I know the hair quality is good?
All our human hair is 100% ethically sourced with cuticles aligned and intact — never mixed with synthetic fibers. Every batch is inspected and stylist-tested before listing, and we back it with our 100% authenticity guarantee. True-to-length, minimal shedding and long-lasting shine are standard.
How should I care for my wig?
Detangle gently from ends to roots before washing. Use a sulfate-free shampoo in cool water, deep condition weekly, and always air-dry on a wig stand. Apply a heat protectant before styling and store in a satin bag to preserve softness and shape.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Every transaction is processed over an encrypted 256-bit SSL connection — your payment details are never stored on our servers.
How do I track my order?
Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with a tracking link. You can also view real-time order status any time by logging into My Account → Order Tracking.
