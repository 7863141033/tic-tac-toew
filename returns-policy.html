Return Policy

14-Day Returns

We want you to love your LuxeHaus order. If something isn't right, you may return any item within 14 days of delivery for a refund or exchange.

Eligibility

  • Items must be unworn, unwashed and in their original packaging.
  • All lace, tags and protective nets must be intact and in place.
  • Hair that has been cut, colored, bleached, installed or styled cannot be returned.
  • For hygiene reasons, lashes, pierced jewelry and opened skincare/makeup are final sale.

How to Start a Return

Email support@luxehausbeauty.com with your order number and reason. We'll reply within one business day with a prepaid return label and instructions. Exchanges ship free; return shipping is deducted from refunds unless the item was defective.

Refunds

Refunds are processed to your original payment method within 5–7 business days of us receiving and inspecting your return. Original shipping fees are non-refundable.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your item arrives damaged or you received the wrong product, contact us within 48 hours with photos and we'll send a replacement immediately — at no cost to you.
