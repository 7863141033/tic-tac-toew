Shipping Policy

Processing Time

All orders are processed within 24 hours of purchase (Monday–Saturday, excluding holidays). You will receive a confirmation email immediately after purchase and a tracking number as soon as your package ships.

Delivery Options & Rates

  • Standard Shipping (5–8 business days) — $5.99, free on all orders over $75.
  • Express Shipping (3–4 business days) — $14.99.
  • Priority Overnight (1–2 business days) — $24.99.

International Shipping

We ship worldwide. International delivery takes 7–14 business days depending on destination and customs processing. Any duties or import taxes are the responsibility of the recipient.

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, a tracking link is emailed to you. You can also track your order at any time through My Account.

Lost or Delayed Packages

If your package is marked delivered but hasn't arrived, or tracking hasn't updated for 7+ days, contact us at support@luxehausbeauty.com within 30 days and we'll make it right — including replacement or refund where appropriate.
